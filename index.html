About Me

🎓 MSc Biomathematics · Eduardo Mondlane University

I am Dário Maxaieie, a Mozambican mathematician and researcher passionate about Artificial Intelligence and Data Science. I graduated as the top student of my class in Mathematics and am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Applied Mathematics with a focus on Mathematical Biology and epidemic disease modelling.

I combine a strong mathematical foundation — Advanced Linear Algebra, Partial Differential Equations, Stochastic Processes, Optimal Control — with hands-on experience in Computer Vision, Machine Learning and Deep Learning through international competitive projects.

I believe AI has the power to transform Africa. I work on projects involving crop anomaly detection, satellite image classification (CubeSat) and mathematical modelling of epidemic diseases.
